Forex Trading

A quantitative look at Smart Money Concepts: liquidity sweeps, order blocks and fair value gaps, detected live on a simulated feed.

Step 1

Mark liquidity

Equal highs and lows, previous day and session extremes — where stop orders sit.

Step 2

Wait for the sweep

Price takes the liquidity, then closes back inside the range and shows displacement.

Step 3

Enter at the zone

Order block or fair value gap left behind by the move becomes the entry area.

Step 4

Define the risk

Stop beyond the sweep, target the opposite liquidity, risk a fixed fraction of the account.
